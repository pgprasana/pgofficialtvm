Cookies Policy

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help enhance your user experience by remembering your preferences, login sessions, and tracking website performance.

How We Use Cookies

PG Official TVM uses cookies to:

  • Analyze website traffic and performance
  • Remember user preferences and sessions
  • Support marketing and promotional efforts

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for basic site operation (e.g., login, checkout).
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand user behavior and improve performance.
  • Preference Cookies: Store your settings such as language or location.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used for promotional campaigns and personalized ads.

Your Choices
